1. Hear what SceneVoice does

Three sample descriptions, the way they come during a video. Nothing is sent anywhere and no key is needed.

If you hear nothing after pressing Hear a sample, your screen reader may not read automatic announcements in this browser: choose “Built-in voice” above and press the button again.

2. Add a key of your own

The descriptions are written by an AI model, and the model needs an API key of your own: a Gemini key from Google AI Studio, free, for YouTube; or a Qwen key from Alibaba Cloud Model Studio, for Bilibili and wherever Google cannot be reached. You paste it in once. It is stored only on this computer and sent only to that service; nothing goes through SceneVoice.
